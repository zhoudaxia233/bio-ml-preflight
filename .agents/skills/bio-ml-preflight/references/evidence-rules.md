# Evidence rules

- `SUPPORTED`: strong controlled baseline evidence under the named realistic split with acceptable dispersion.
- `SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITS`: moderate or sensitivity-limited evidence.
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`: weak signal, weak permutation separation, or unstable decision.
- `CONTRADICTED`: evidence directly conflicts with the proposed boundary, such as random success and cold-entity collapse.
- `NOT_ASSESSABLE`: a required target, unit, metadata field, metric, or feasible split is absent.

Report supporting evidence, opposing evidence, uncertainty, unmet assumptions, underlying values, and one cheap next evidence step. Counts of entities and replicates are transparent proxies, not exact effective sample sizes.
